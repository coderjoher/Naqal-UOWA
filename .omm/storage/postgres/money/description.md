Payment (payments): append-only ledger; type subscription|cash_fare|tier_difference, method cash_office|cash_driver|zaincash|qi, amount whole IQD (negative = reversal), receiptNo unique per university, studentId, collectedById, externalRef, reversesPaymentId (unique), runId, rideRequestId (unique), taxiRideId (unique), idempotencyKey (unique), note.
