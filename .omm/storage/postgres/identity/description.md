RosterEntry (roster_entries, unique universityId+studentId): students imported by the office for the manual identity provider (identity/roster.provider.ts), with a bcrypt-hashed one-time activation code, expiry, attempt counter and activatedAt. OtpCode (otp_codes, keyed by phone, NOT tenant-scoped because the university is chosen after sign-in): bcrypt hash of the driver's 6-digit SMS code, expiresAt (5 min), attempts (dies after 5); deleted when consumed.
