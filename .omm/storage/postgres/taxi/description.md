TaxiRide (taxi_rides): one campus taxi trip; studentId, driverId (null until accepted), direction to_campus|from_campus, the student's lat/lng/label, distanceKm, fare fixed at request, status requested|accepted|arrived|on_trip|done|cancelled|expired, unique clientId (retry-safe booking), expiresAt and per-state timestamps, cancelledBy. Indexes (universityId, status, createdAt), (studentId, createdAt), (driverId, createdAt). The collected fare is a Payment with taxiRideId (one per ride). Live driver positions are NOT stored here; they live in the Redis hash taxi:online:{uni}.
