Settlement (settlements, unique universityId+month): status draft|approved, commissionBp frozen at compute time, per-tier JSON (pool, runs, commission), BigInt totals (totalPool, totalPayout, totalCommission, totalCash, unallocated, roundingResidual), excludedRuns, computedAt, approvedAt/approvedById. SettlementLine (settlement_lines, unique settlementId+driverId): runs, per-tier shares JSON, cash, cashCommission, payout (negative means the driver owes the office). Drafts are upserted and lines replaced on each compute; approved rows are frozen by triggers.
