Notification (notifications): per-user outbox and in-app inbox (kind, data JSON, unique dedupeKey, pushedAt delivery claim, readAt). DeviceToken (device_tokens, unique token): FCM registration per app install. Announcement (announcements): office message to all students, a wave on a date or a gathering point, with recipients count, author and expiresAt; fan-out to recipients is done through Notification rows.
