Per-university configuration managed by the office and super admin. universities/: university records and integrationConfig (super admin). tiers/: distance tiers and prices (tier-rules.ts). gathering-points/: pickup points with coordinates (changes trigger travel-matrix recomputation). driver-requirements/: required driver documents/fields per university. Reads of tiers, points, waves and requirements go through ConfigCache (Redis) and are invalidated on write.
