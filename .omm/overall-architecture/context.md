Multi-tenant by design: every tenant table carries universityId and is filtered automatically by a Prisma extension (NF-05), even though the first deployment serves one university. Four roles exist: student, driver, office, super_admin. The dashboard is used by office staff and the super admin; students and drivers use the Flutter apps (mobile, plus web builds served by nginx on ports 8081/8082). Requirement IDs in code comments (NF-xx, DR-xx, PA-xx, SE-xx, P8/P10) refer to the product requirements document in docs/.
