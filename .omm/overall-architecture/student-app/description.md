Flutter app for students (apps/student_app). Covers onboarding/sign-in against the university identity provider, bus booking per wave, subscriptions and receipts, live bus tracking for the student's own run, on-demand taxi requests, trip history, notifications and feedback. Built for Android/iOS and as a web app (infra/flutter-web, port 8081). Arabic and English via l10n ARB files. Depends on naql_ui, naql_core and naql_app.
