Queue 'routing' (ROUTING_QUEUE). RoutingService.scheduleMatrixRebuild adds job travel-matrix.rebuild with fixed jobId matrix-{universityId} and 2 s delay, so a burst of gathering-point edits collapses into one job (3 attempts, exponential backoff 5 s). Producers: PointsService (point added/moved/toggled) and UniversitiesService (campus moved). TravelMatrixProcessor calls OSRM /table for campus + active points and replaces the tenant's travel_time rows in one transaction (DS-01).
