Realtime channel replacing Laravel Reverb from the PRD. LiveGateway (@WebSocketGateway namespace '/live', websocket + polling) authenticates sockets by JWT and places them in rooms; LiveHub is the thin injectable emitter that services (DispatchEngine, RunsService, TaxiService, NotificationsService, LiveService) call after commit. With RedisIoAdapter, an emit on any instance reaches sockets on all instances (NF-01).
