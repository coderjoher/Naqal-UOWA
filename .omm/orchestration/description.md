Asynchronous and event-driven plumbing of the Naqal API: BullMQ queues and workers (dispatch, taxi, routing) that keep planning, waitlist rechecks, expiries and matrix rebuilds off the request path (NF-03); the Socket.IO /live gateway with its LiveHub emitter and rooms for realtime bus, run, taxi and notification events; the transactional notification outbox delivered over sockets and FCM; and the in-process stale-while-revalidate cache for office read models that every dispatch/run change invalidates. Redis backs both BullMQ and the Socket.IO adapter.
