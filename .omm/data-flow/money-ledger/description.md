All money enters the system through one path: PaymentsService.record (apps/api/src/payments/payments.service.ts), called inside the caller's transaction by three producers: the office selling a monthly subscription (SubscriptionsService.create, type subscription, method cash_office), a bus driver confirming a pay-per-ride fare (RunsService.fare, type cash_fare, method cash_driver) and a taxi trip ending (TaxiService.end, cash_fare/cash_driver). record() asks the PaymentProvider to confirm collection, takes the next gap-free receipt number from receipt_counters and inserts an immutable Payment row. Corrections are PaymentsService.reverse: a new negative row pointing at the original (and the linked subscription is cancelled). Receipts are rendered as PDF on demand.
