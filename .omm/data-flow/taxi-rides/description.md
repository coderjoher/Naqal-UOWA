Campus taxi (P10, apps/api/src/taxi/). A student gets a quote (OSRM distance to campus, university tariff), then requests a TaxiRide with a client id; the fare is fixed at request time. The ride is offered to nearby, free, online taxi drivers found in the Redis hash taxi:online:{uni} (socket `taxi:offer` card with a coarse area + `taxi.offer` push). The first driver to accept wins via a conditional update; the driver then arrives, starts and ends the trip, forwarding its position to the student on each heartbeat. Ending the trip records a cash_fare Payment in the same transaction. A BullMQ sweep every 20 s expires requests nobody accepted.
