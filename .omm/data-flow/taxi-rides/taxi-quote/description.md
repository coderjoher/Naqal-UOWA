TaxiService.quote (apps/api/src/taxi/taxi.service.ts). Requires University.taxiEnabled and the point inside the coverage polygon; gets road distance and duration to campus from RoutingService.toCampus (OSRM, approximate fallback); fare = fareFor(distanceKm, tariff) = max(minFare, baseFare + ceil(km x perKm)) rounded up to the fare step (taxi-rules.ts). Also reports how many online taxis are nearby and the nearest one's pickup ETA. Nothing is stored.
