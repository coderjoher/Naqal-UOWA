TaxiProcessor on BullMQ queue `taxi` (apps/api/src/taxi/taxi.processor.ts): job scheduler `taxi-sweep` every 20 s (off with DISPATCH_TICK=off) calls TaxiService.expireDue, which finds requested rides past expiresAt across all universities (runAsSystem), flips each to expired with a conditional update plus a `taxi.expired` notification, delivers it, and emits `taxi:gone` / `taxi:ride`.
