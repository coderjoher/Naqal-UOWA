Read-side aggregation of the immutable inputs into monthly money and KPIs (apps/api/src/settlement/, apps/api/src/reports/). SettlementService computes a draft per university and month from active subscriptions (tier pools), counted runs (GPS-verified or office verdict true) and net cash fares (bus and taxi, reversals included), using the integer formula in payout.ts; writes Settlement + SettlementLine rows; the office reviews flagged runs, approves (audited, then immutable) and exports PDF/XLSX. Drivers see estimates via GET /drivers/me/earnings; super admins see revenue by payment type per university (GET admin/overview). ReportsService (TO-10) computes the monthly report (requests, no-shows, punctuality, subscriber retention, cash fares per tier) on the fly.
