ReportsService.report (apps/api/src/reports/reports.service.ts, metrics.ts computeReport; served from feedback.controller.ts, also as CSV via reportCsv). For a month (optionally one tier): loads tiers, RideRequests, non-cancelled Runs with waves, this and last month's active Subscriptions, and cash_driver Payments linked to the month's ride requests; computes subscribers, requests, served, fulfilment rate, on-time rate, waitlist-expiry rate, renewal rate, runs and revenue per tier in memory. Uses settlementMonthRange so reports and settlement cover the same days. Nothing is stored.
