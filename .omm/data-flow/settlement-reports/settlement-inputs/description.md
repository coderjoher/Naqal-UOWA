SettlementService.calculate (apps/api/src/settlement/settlement.service.ts). For the month range: loads non-cancelled Runs (re-running verifyRun for those without an office verdict when computing), groups active Subscriptions by tier into pools (sum of price), sums cash_driver Payments on this month's runs and on taxi rides that ended in the month, netting reversals via `reverses`, and counts verified runs per driver and tier. Nothing is written by calculate itself.
