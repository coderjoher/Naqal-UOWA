LiveService.ingest (apps/api/src/live/live.service.ts). Sanitizes points, checks ownership/status against a 15 s cached route, then in one Redis pipeline tries `SET live:pos:{runId}:{minute} NX EX 6h` per point and `SET live:near:{runId}:{seq|campus} NX` when within 120 m of a stop or 300 m of campus. Points that won any slot are bulk-inserted as RunPosition rows. If the newest point is newer than live:last:{runId}, it updates the hash (24 h TTL), GEOADDs to live:geo:{uni}, broadcasts via LiveHub and calls NotificationsService.addNow with `eta` drafts (deduplicated once per ride).
