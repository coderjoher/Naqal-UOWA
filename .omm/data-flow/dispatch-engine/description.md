Turns open RideRequests into Runs (one bus, one wave, one date) with ordered RunStops and ETAs, and maintains the waitlist (apps/api/src/dispatch/). DispatchProcessor (BullMQ queue `dispatch`) ticks every minute and queues `wave.plan` for waves within 60 minutes of departure; `waitlist.recheck` / `waitlist.expire` jobs re-seat or expire waiting students. DispatchEngine loads the wave state inside a transaction holding a per-wave/date advisory lock, calls the pure domain (dispatch/domain/: planWave, insertRequest, recheckWaitlist, cancelRequest, moveRequest, addExtraRun), writes the result back, appends Notification outbox rows, and after commit schedules expiry jobs, emits `run:updated` to drivers/ops and delivers notifications.
