Pure, database-free dispatch algorithms (apps/api/src/dispatch/domain/: dispatch.ts, insert.ts, schedule.ts, types.ts). planWave groups open passengers by gender into buses within driver seat capacity and builds stop order/times that still reach campus by the wave time; insertRequest/recheckWaitlist seat late requests into existing runs or keep them waitlisted until expiry; cancelRequest frees a seat unless the bus passed the stop; moveRequest/addExtraRun support office overrides. Returns new runs plus DispatchEvents (assigned, waitlisted, bumped, expired). Covered by property, simulation and fixture tests (*.spec.ts, test-city.ts).
