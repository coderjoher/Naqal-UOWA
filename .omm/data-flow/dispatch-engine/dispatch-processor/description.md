BullMQ worker for the `dispatch` queue (apps/api/src/dispatch/dispatch.processor.ts). On start it upserts a job scheduler `wave-tick` (every 60 s, disabled with DISPATCH_TICK=off). tick() runs as system (cross-tenant) and finds active waves for today and tomorrow (Baghdad) that are within PLAN_LEAD_MIN = 60 minutes of departure and have no WavePlan, then enqueues `wave.plan` with a deterministic jobId `plan-{waveId}-{date}`. It dispatches `wave.plan` to DispatchEngine.plan and `waitlist.recheck` / `waitlist.expire` to DispatchEngine.recheck.
