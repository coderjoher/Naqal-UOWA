The Redis-backed BullMQ queue `dispatch` (prefix QUEUE_PREFIX, default `naql`). Job types: `wave.tick` (repeatable scheduler), `wave.plan` (deduplicated by jobId, 3 attempts, exponential backoff 2 s), `waitlist.recheck` (enqueued after a new request on a planned wave, a cancellation or a move; 3 attempts, backoff 1 s), `waitlist.expire` (delayed until a waitlisted request's waitlistedUntil). Payload is a WaveRef {universityId, waveId, date}. Completed jobs are removed; last 100 failures kept.
