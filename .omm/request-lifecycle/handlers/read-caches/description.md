Two caches on the read path. ConfigCache (config-cache/config-cache.service.ts): Redis key cfg:{universityId}:{tiers|points|waves|driver-requirements}, 1 h TTL, best-effort (Redis errors fall back to the loader), invalidated on config writes. officeViews (common/swr-cache.ts): in-process SwrCache with 5 s freshness and max 200 entries; dispatch board `{uid}:board:{date}` and live ops `{uid}:ops:{date}`. Stale entries are served immediately while one background refresh runs; concurrent misses share one load. Student/driver activity marks entries stale (soft invalidate), office actions drop them (hard invalidate) so the office sees its own change at once.
