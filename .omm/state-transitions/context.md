Enums live in apps/api/prisma/schema.prisma (RideStatus, RunStatus, TaxiRideStatus, DriverStatus, SubscriptionStatus). Transition rules are kept as pure functions where possible so they can be unit-tested: nextRunState/stopDeparture (runs/run-rules.ts), nextTaxiState (taxi/taxi-rules.ts), nextStatus/editable (drivers/driver-rules.ts). Ride request transitions are driven by the dispatch domain (dispatch/domain/dispatch.ts) and persisted by DispatchEngine.save() inside a per-wave advisory-locked transaction.
