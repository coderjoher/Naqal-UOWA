Subscription.status (enum SubscriptionStatus: active | cancelled), monthly subscription (ST-03, TO-06). SubscriptionsService.create (subscriptions/subscriptions.service.ts) records the cash payment and creates the subscription as active in one transaction, covering a calendar month (period-policy.ts). The only transition is active -> cancelled, done by PaymentsService reversal (payments/payments.service.ts): a negative reversal payment is inserted and subscriptions linked to the original payment are set cancelled. Time-based states (expiring, expired) are not stored; they are derived in forStudent().
