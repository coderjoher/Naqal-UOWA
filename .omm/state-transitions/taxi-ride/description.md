TaxiRide.status (enum TaxiRideStatus), P10 campus taxis (TX-01..07). Rules are the pure nextTaxiState(status, action) in taxi/taxi-rules.ts with actions accept | arrive | start | end | cancel_student | cancel_driver | expire; TaxiService (taxi/taxi.service.ts) applies them through transition(), a conditional updateMany on (id, status, driverId). ACTIVE_TAXI = requested, accepted, arrived, on_trip; a driver is busy in accepted/arrived/on_trip. Every transition emits LiveHub.taxiRide ('taxi:ride') to the student, driver and ops room; leaving requested (accept, cancel, expire) also emits taxiGone ('taxi:gone') to the university's taxi room so other drivers drop the offer card.
