`/settlement` (pages/SettlementPage.tsx): monthly driver settlement (defaults to last Baghdad month). Office computes a draft, reviews runs flagged by GPS verification (not_completed, no_gps, ended_elsewhere, too_fast, teleport, off_path) and records a verdict, then approves. Shows totals for payout, cash kept by drivers and cash commission. Backed by apps/api/src/settlement. Office only.
