Turn-by-turn navigation is delegated to third-party apps (DR-06). apps/driver_app/lib/data/run_controller.dart builds `google.navigation:q=lat,lng&mode=d` (Android) or `https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=...&travelmode=driving`, and `https://waze.com/ul?ll=lat,lng&navigate=yes`; run_screen.dart opens them through urlLauncherProvider, falling back to the web Google Maps URL if the Android intent fails.
