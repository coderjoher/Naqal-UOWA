Self-hosted routing engine in three services sharing the `osrm` volume: `osrm-download` (curl the Geofabrik Iraq extract once), `osrm-prepare` (ghcr.io/project-osrm/osrm-backend:v5.27.1 running infra/osrm/prepare.sh) and `osrm` (osrm-routed --algorithm mld --max-table-size 1000 on :5000). Consumed by apps/api/src/routing/osrm.client.ts for route legs and travel-time matrices (dispatch, ETA).
