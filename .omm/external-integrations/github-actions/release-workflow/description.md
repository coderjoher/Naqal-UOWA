`Release` (release.yml; `v*` tags or manual, P8). `android` job builds signed app bundles for both apps with API_URL, SENTRY_DSN, MAP_TILES and MAP_ATTRIBUTION from secrets and uploads to the Google Play internal track; `testflight` job on macos-15 imports signing certs and the provisioning profile, builds the student app IPA and uploads it to TestFlight. Both skip gracefully when signing secrets are absent.
