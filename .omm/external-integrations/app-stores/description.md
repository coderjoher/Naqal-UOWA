Distribution targets of .github/workflows/release.yml (on `v*` tags or manual): both Flutter apps as signed app bundles to the Google Play internal testing track (r0adkll/upload-google-play, PLAY_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON, keystore from ANDROID_KEYSTORE_BASE64), and the student app as an IPA to TestFlight (apple-actions/upload-testflight-build on macos-15, App Store Connect API key). Jobs skip when the signing secrets are missing (HAS_SECRETS).
