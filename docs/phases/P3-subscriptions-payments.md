# P3 — Subscriptions and cash payments

Status: in-progress
Depends on: P2

## Goal

The office records a cash subscription payment and the student's subscription is active in the app
instantly. Every money record is immutable.

## Scope

- ST-03 Subscription status, tier, price, expiry and pay-at-office instructions
- TO-06 Record cash payment, issue receipt, activate subscription
- PA-02 Price by registered point's tier; farther boarding costs the difference
- PA-04 Provider-agnostic payment model
- NF-14 Immutable money records with audit trail

## Deliverables

- `payments` table with `method` (`cash_office`, `cash_driver`, later `zaincash`, `qi`),
  `type` (`subscription`, `cash_fare`, `tier_difference`, `reversal`), sequential per-university
  `receipt_no` from a `receipt_counters` row (`INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE … RETURNING`).
- Corrections are made with reversal rows only (`POST /payments/:id/reverse` writes a negative
  row and cancels the subscription). A PostgreSQL trigger (`forbid_money_mutation`) rejects
  `UPDATE`/`DELETE` on `payments`; `settlement_lines` gets the same trigger when it is created in P6.
- `PaymentProvider` interface with `OfficeCashProvider`; electronic providers register in
  `PAYMENT_PROVIDERS` and store their id in `external_ref` (no schema change).
- Subscription period (Q4, default until decided): **calendar month in Asia/Baghdad**, in one
  place — `subscriptions/period-policy.ts`. Refunds (Q5) are not supported in v1.
- Pricing (`subscriptions/pricing.ts`): subscription price = tier of the student's registered
  point; boarding at a farther tier costs the difference of the tiers' ride prices, nearer costs 0
  (charged per ride from P4).
- Receipt: A6 PDF generated by the API with pdfkit and the bundled IBM Plex Sans Arabic font
  (`GET /payments/:id/receipt.pdf`), Arabic right-to-left.
- Dashboard: Subscriptions page — find student → price preview → record payment → download
  receipt; month list with reversal.
- Student app: Subscription card (status pill, tier, price, expiry, office location note) on Home.
  It refreshes every 4 s while not active, so activation shows within 5 s. Push notifications
  come with the notification service in P5.
